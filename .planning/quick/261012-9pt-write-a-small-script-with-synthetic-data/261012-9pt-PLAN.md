## ClassScore field names (locked from evaluation.py)

Use these exact names in EXPECTED dicts and comparisons (not `f1_score` / `true_positives`):

- `precision`, `recall`, `f1`, `tp`, `fp`, `fn`

Default `evaluate(...)` criteria: `SameClass` + `SpanIoU(0.5)` (no `match_attributes` unless a case needs it).

## Suggested synthetic scenario (discretion — keep or refine if counts stay hand-checkable)

Two document_ids, two classes (`condition`, `medication`), default IoU matching:

1. `doc-perfect` — identical predicted/gold `condition` span → contributes TP to `condition`.
2. `doc-miss` — predicted has a `medication` span gold lacks (FP); gold has a different `medication` span prediction lacks (FN); optionally one overlapping `condition` that matches (TP).

Hand-compute EXPECTED before coding, then encode as structured dicts beside the case builders. Example shape after aggregation (adjust if you change spans):

- `condition`: tp/fp/fn such that f1 is exact and obvious
- `medication`: tp=0 with non-zero fp and fn → f1=0.0
- `micro`: sum of per-class triples → known P/R/F1 via `prf_from_counts` rules (zero denominator → 0.0)

Do not invent cases that need `match_attributes` or custom `text_eval_func` unless you also annotate those expectations.
